What is a Monte Carlo simulation?

Used by banks, funds and CFA charterholders to measure risk.

A way to answer 'what could happen?' without guessing one future. The computer tries thousands of random scenarios and shows you the pattern. Like rolling a die 10,000 times to see which numbers come up, instead of predicting one roll. The name comes from the Monte Carlo casino.
